Hyperlocal Laundry Pickup & Delivery Platform — Kakkanad
 
Design and build a premium, modern, highly user-friendly laundry pickup and delivery platform initially targeting Kakkanad, Kochi, Kerala.
 
This is NOT a generic laundry website.
 
The product is an end-to-end hyperlocal laundry management platform connecting:
 
Customer → Pickup Driver → Laundry Partner → Delivery Driver → Customer
 
The most important product principles are:
 
Simplicity + Trust + Transparency + Convenience + Visibility
 
The UI should make the customer feel:
 
“I know what I ordered, what it costs, where my clothes are, and when I will get them back.”

37. MOBILE UX
 
Optimize for one-handed use.
 
Primary CTA should be easy to reach.
 
Bottom sheets should be used where appropriate.
 
Avoid tiny buttons.
 
Do not require horizontal scrolling.
 
Booking should ideally take less than one minute for a returning user.

tracked bags
 
transparent pricing

39. LOCALIZATION
 
Initially support:

Architect the UI so Malayalam can be added later.
 
Do not hard-code strings directly into components.
 
Use a translation structure.
 
 
40. DATA-DRIVEN UI
 
Prices, services, slots, subscription plans, zones, offers and statuses should be treated as backend data.
 
Do NOT hard-code business data into the UI.
 
Use realistic mock data during frontend development.
 
Structure API integration so mock data can later be replaced with real APIs without rewriting components.
 
 
41. IMPORTANT PRODUCT RULE
 
The customer should NEVER see internal operational complexity unless necessary.
 
Internally:
 
Partner Driver Bag SLA Processing Settlement Capacity
 
Externally:
 
Your clothes are being cleaned.
 
Your clothes are ready.
 
Your driver is on the way.
 
Your order will arrive tomorrow.
 
Keep the customer experience simple.
